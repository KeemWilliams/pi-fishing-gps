Wind on a fishing map: the compass and the wind dial

Wind shows up in two places: one compass on the map, and a dial on the Today screen. Both say whether the number is a forecast or a measurement, and neither pretends the wind was measured at the boat.

Built Works on demo data. · Last changed 2026-10-28

Two views of the same number

The Today screen has a wind tile. A small compass dial shows which way the wind is blowing, the tile gives a speed and a direction in plain words ("SW 12 mph"), the gusts, and one sentence that says it back: "Wind from the southwest, 12 mph, gusts 18". The label on the tile says whether the value is a forecast. In the screenshot it is the National Weather Service hourly forecast for one point, and the tile says "Forecast. One point, not measured at your boat."

When the boat is moving fast enough for the GPS course to be believed, the tile adds where the wind is relative to the boat, for example "Wind on your back right". When the boat is slow or stopped the course is not trustworthy, so the tile leaves that phrase out and does not guess.

Wind on the map

The Wind chip on the map adds a single small compass in the lower corner with the speed, the gusts and a short note ("Not at your boat"). I first drew a field of wind arrows across the map and removed it. A field of arrows implied that wind was known everywhere on the water, and what I have is a forecast for a point or a reading from a buoy.

Where it comes from

Forecast wind comes from the National Weather Service hourly forecast, with Open-Meteo as a second forecast source. Observed wind comes from NOAA buoys and coastal stations when one is near enough. The app records the source and the age of each wind value and shows both.

Where wind matters in the bite score

In the coastal data I tested, wind lowered the chance of a catch by about 2 points over its normal range, and it did so on boat trips only. Shore and pier trips showed no wind effect. So the bite score applies a wind penalty only when the device is in boat or kayak mode. The details and the chart are on the bite score page.

Limits

  • A forecast for one point can be wrong by a lot on open water, near shore and under storms.
  • A buoy reading is a point too, and it can be kilometres from where the boat is.
  • Gust figures are forecast gusts, not measured ones, unless the tile says observed.
  • Wind never replaces a marine forecast or the NWS warnings. The app shows warnings in their own full-screen alert.
