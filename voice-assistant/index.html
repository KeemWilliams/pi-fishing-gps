Offline voice assistant for fishing

You can ask the unit questions such as what the water temperature is or what is biting. The answer is built from the device's own data, checked against it before it is shown, and listed with its sources.

Partly built Typed questions work on demo data. Speech needs two programs and their models, which are not installed. · Last changed 2026-10-28

What works now

The Ask screen takes typed questions and gives a short written answer. In the screenshot the answer is "The water at the probe is 62.5 F and rising about 0.6 F an hour. The nearest buoy, 44058, reads 61 F and is 40 minutes old." Under it, two chips name where each number came from and when it was read. The suggested questions below change with context.

The screen also says that talking to the unit needs a speech service that is not installed yet. That is accurate.

Answers come from tools, not from memory

The assistant does not look things up in its head. A question is matched to a tool, such as water temperature, tide, alerts, species rules or spot ranking, and the tool returns data with a source and an age. The answer is written from that data.

Then a grounding check runs. Every number, unit, time and date in the candidate answer must be backed by what the tools returned this turn, within a rounding tolerance. The check also rejects an answer that leaves out an active warning, hides how old stale data is, or quotes a depth without the chart caveat. If a candidate fails, the unit speaks a fixed template instead.

Two behaviours I care about:

  • If the unit cannot confirm the weather alerts, the answer to "is it safe to fish?" is a fixed "I can't confirm alerts". It never says "no storms expected" on missing data.
  • When the bite score is explained, only inputs that moved the score are mentioned. The moon and solunar windows are shown on screen but carry no weight, so the assistant cannot say they helped.

The language model is optional and off by default

Out of the box the assistant uses templates and a sentence matcher (hassil), with no language model at all. The configuration allows a model to phrase answers, either a small local model served by llama.cpp or an endpoint reached over a network, including a hosted one. It is off in the shipped settings, and I have not chosen a model.

If a model is ever pointed at an off-device endpoint, a redaction layer runs first. It rounds the current position to 0.1 degree, removes every other coordinate, replaces spot names and ids with per-turn tokens, and keeps catch history to date, species, count, lure and conditions. The grounding check then works from the redacted data, so the answer can only quote what the model was allowed to see.

Speech

The speech path is written. The voice service takes an uploaded utterance, converts it to 16 kHz audio, sends it to a whisper.cpp server on the Pi, passes the text to the assistant, and speaks the answer with Piper. The service degrades by part: with no speech-to-text it refuses audio, and with no Piper the text answers still work.

None of that has run, because the whisper.cpp binary, its model and a Piper voice are separate installs I have not made. Microphone capture and speaker playback on the handheld are also not written. A spoken question that gets a spoken answer in 8 seconds is the target, and it is untested.

Not done

  • Speech-to-text and text-to-speech models are not installed.
  • No language model is chosen or installed.
  • Fishing vocabulary and Virginia place names need tuning for speech recognition. The plan is a vocabulary prompt first and fine-tuning only if keyword errors stay high.
