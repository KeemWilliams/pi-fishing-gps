Safety and limits: not for navigation

This is a hobby project. It is not for navigation, it has not been tested on the water, and some of what it shows is a prediction or a guess. This page lists the limits in plain terms.

Last changed 2026-10-28

Not for navigation

Do not use this project to steer a boat, find a channel, avoid a hazard or decide whether a spot is deep enough. The maps on this site show an invented river. Depth data in a real pack would be chart data with the datum unknown, and the chart data it would come from is not a navigation product. The app says "Not for navigation" on its map screen and I would keep it that way in any version.

Carry proper charts or a chartplotter you trust, a marine radio and a way to call for help. The unit does not share your location and is not a rescue device.

Predictions are not measurements

  • Tidal current arrows and tide times are NOAA predictions for a station. Real current at your boat can differ, especially after rain or in wind.
  • Forecast wind is a forecast for a point, not a reading at your boat. The app labels forecast and observed values differently.
  • A forecast that has not refreshed for 48 hours is labelled "may be wrong", with its age. If alerts cannot be checked, the app says it cannot confirm them. It does not say all is clear.

Weather alerts

A severe thunderstorm warning is meant to produce fixed wording ("Leave the water now"), a full screen overlay that only the Acknowledge button closes, and a buzzer pattern that sounds even in quiet hours. The software for this is written and tested on a laptop. None of it has been tested on a Pi, with a real buzzer, outdoors. Until it has, the plan for any trip is to check the National Weather Service forecast on a phone before leaving, and to treat the unit as a second opinion.

The bite score is an aid, not an oracle

The bite score rests on 58,254 recorded trips, and it is a judgement informed by that data. Water temperature is the main factor. A "slow" rating has not been shown to be a day you should skip, and a "prime" rating is not a promise. Details and caveats are on the bite score page.

Fish ID and regulations

The fish identifier ranks candidates and can be wrong, including between look-alikes that have different size limits. Confirm the species yourself. The app's regulation table for Virginia was a placeholder at my last check, until I verify each row against the state's pages, so a "can I keep it" answer should be read as a check-the-source answer. Use the official Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources and Virginia Marine Resources Commission pages for limits and seasons.

Things that could still go wrong

  • A map pack with a wrong or missing label, or a place marked unverified that I have not visited.
  • A sensor that is unplugged and shows no reading. The design shows a gap rather than a number, and I have not verified that on hardware.
  • Heat, sunlight, salt and water on the electronics. The first trips are planned as short, near shore, with a phone signal and a backup phone navigation app.

If you spot a mistake on this site, please open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
